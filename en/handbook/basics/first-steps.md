A page without anything special — that is exactly what it is for. It shows what a normal note looks
like in this layout when it is neither long nor short.

## One section

The text takes the middle column of the frame. On the right are the table of contents and the
backlinks, on the left the navigation. On a tablet the right column moves below the text, on a
phone everything stands one below the other.
