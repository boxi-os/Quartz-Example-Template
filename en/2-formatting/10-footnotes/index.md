Notes that do not interrupt the sentence — and text that never reaches the page at all.

## The pages

- [[en/2-formatting/10-footnotes/basics|Footnotes — the basic form]] — A marker in the text, the text at the foot of the page.
- [[en/2-formatting/10-footnotes/comments|Comments]] — Text that only exists in the source.
- [[en/2-formatting/10-footnotes/variants|Named and repeated footnotes]] — Names instead of numbers, several references, footnotes inline.
