## Dashes

```md
Hyphen: Quartz-based
En dash: 2020–2026
Em dash: like — this
```

Hyphen: Quartz-based
En dash: 2020–2026
Em dash: like — this

> [!note] The rule of this template
> In English text the em dash is set with spaces around it — like in this sentence. For ranges of
> numbers the en dash is used without spaces: 10–20.
