A page's apparatus: not the content, but what lies around it. Two of them stand to the right of the
text — table of contents and graph — the other two as a pair of boxes directly below it.

## The pages

- [[en/5-design/04-beside-the-content/backlinks|Backlinks]] — What points at this page.
- [[en/5-design/04-beside-the-content/recent-notes|Recently changed]] — The most recent notes as a box below the text.
- [[en/5-design/04-beside-the-content/table-of-contents|Table of contents]] — The outline on the right — every level, not just three.
- [[en/5-design/04-beside-the-content/graph|The graph]] — The surroundings of a note as a network.
